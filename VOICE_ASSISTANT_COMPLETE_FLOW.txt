Scenario: User asks Ruby about high blood pressure
---------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: User says "Hey Ruby"
└─> STT detects wake word
    └─> TTS speaks: "Hey, I am Ruby. How can I help you?"
        └─> User hears Ruby's greeting


STEP 2: User asks question (within 5 seconds)
└─> User: "What is high blood pressure?"
    └─> STT records audio for 5 seconds
        └─> Vosk transcribes: "what is high blood pressure"
            └─> Console: ✅ [COMMAND] 'what is high blood pressure'


STEP 3: Command sent to OpenAI GPT
└─> LLM receives: "what is high blood pressure"
    └─> GPT processes with Ruby's personality
        └─> GPT generates senior-friendly response
            └─> Console: 💬 Ruby: 'High blood pressure means...'


STEP 4: Ruby speaks response
└─> TTS (gTTS/pyttsx3) converts text to speech
    └─> User hears: "High blood pressure means your blood pushes 
                      too hard against your blood vessel walls..."
        └─> Response takes ~5-10 seconds to speak


STEP 5: Back to listening
└─> Console: 🎧 Listening for 'hey ruby'...
    └─> Ready for next interaction

================================================================================
CONFIGURATION SUMMARY
================================================================================

Wake Word Detection (STT)
--------------------------
- Wake word: "hey ruby"
- Model: Vosk (offline)
- Command timeout: 5 seconds
- Sample rate: 16000 Hz
- Audio source: USB/Bluetooth microphone

LLM Processing
--------------
- Provider: OpenAI
- Model: GPT-3.5-Turbo
- Max response: 150 tokens (~100 words)
- Temperature: 0.7 (balanced creativity)
- Timeout: 10 seconds
- Context: Stateless (no memory)

System Prompt:
"You are Ruby, a helpful voice assistant for senior citizens. Be 
friendly, clear, and concise, super concise, and avoid using hard 
jargons and vocabularies, use easy words. You can only provide 
information and clarification. You CANNOT execute actions, create 
reminders, or control devices."

Text-to-Speech (TTS)
---------------------
- Primary: gTTS (online, high quality)
- Fallback: pyttsx3 (offline, basic quality)
- Voice: Female (when available)
- Rate: Slightly slower for seniors
- Output: Bluetooth speaker or system audio

----------------------------

Scenario 3: STT Microphone Failure
-----------------------------------
Console: ❌ Error checking audio devices
Server won't start STT service
(Voice commands won't work)


Scenario 4: TTS Speaker Failure
--------------------------------
Console: ⚠️  TTS not available
LLM still processes, but no audio output
(Response printed to console only)


Scenario 5: Wake Word Not Detected
-----------------------------------
👤 User: "Hey Ruby" (unclear/too quiet)
(Nothing happens, keeps listening)
Try speaking louder or closer to microphone

WHAT RUBY CANNOT DO
================================================================================

❌ Execute Actions
- Cannot set reminders
- Cannot make phone calls
- Cannot control smart home devices
- Cannot send messages

❌ Access Real-Time Data
- Cannot check actual weather
- Cannot look up current time
- Cannot access your calendar
- Cannot check live medication schedules

❌ Access Personal Information
- Cannot see your medical records
- Cannot know your medication list
- Cannot access your family contacts
- Cannot view your reminders

NOTE: Ruby only uses the knowledge from GPT's training data.
Ruby provides information and clarification only.

TIPS FOR BEST EXPERIENCE
================================================================================

For Users:
----------
✅ Speak clearly and at moderate pace
✅ Keep questions simple and direct
✅ Wait for Ruby's greeting before asking
✅ Stay near microphone (within 1-2 feet)
✅ Reduce background noise
✅ Speak slightly louder than normal conversation

❌ Don't speak too fast
❌ Don't use complex medical terms
❌ Don't expect Ruby to do actions
❌ Don't expect Ruby to remember previous conversations


For Developers:
---------------
✅ Monitor console for errors
✅ Check internet connectivity regularly
✅ Keep OpenAI API credits topped up
✅ Test different microphone positions
✅ Adjust command timeout if needed (config.py)
✅ Consider conversation logging for debugging
