🎤 TWO MODES OF INTERACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mode 1: TEXT CONVERSATION - "Hey Ruby"
---------------------------------------
Wake Word: "Hey Ruby"
Model: GPT-3.5-Turbo
Cost: ~$0.0002 per conversation
Speed: ~1-2 seconds

Flow:
1. User: "Hey Ruby"
2. Ruby: "Hey, I am Ruby. How can I help you?"
3. User: "What is diabetes?"
4. Ruby: (answers using text-based knowledge)

Use For:
✅ General health questions
✅ Information and clarification
✅ Explanations and advice
✅ Fast responses


Mode 2: VISION CONVERSATION - "Watch Ruby"
-------------------------------------------
Wake Word: "Watch Ruby"
Model: GPT-4o (multimodal)
Cost: ~$0.002-0.003 per conversation (10-15x more expensive)
Speed: ~2-5 seconds

Flow:
1. User: "Watch Ruby"
2. Camera captures current frame
3. Ruby: "I'm looking, go ahead"
4. User: "What pill is this?"
5. Ruby: (analyzes image + answers question)

Use For:
✅ Medicine/pill identification help
✅ Reading labels and text
✅ Food identification
✅ Object recognition
✅ When you need to SEE something

================================================================================
TECHNICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

Wake Word Detection:
--------------------
- Engine: Vosk (offline)
- Sample Rate: 16000 Hz
- Wake Words: "hey ruby" (text) | "watch ruby" (vision)
- Timeout: 5 seconds per command
- Real-time transcription feedback

Text LLM:
---------
- Model: GPT-3.5-Turbo
- Max Tokens: 150
- Temperature: 0.7
- Timeout: 10 seconds
- System: Senior-friendly, concise, simple language

Vision LLM:
-----------
- Model: GPT-4o (multimodal)
- Max Tokens: 200
- Temperature: 0.7
- Timeout: 15 seconds
- Image Detail: Low (faster, cheaper)
- Format: JPEG, 85% quality
- System: Direct answers, senior-friendly

Camera Integration:
-------------------
- Source: face_detection_service.latest_frame
- Format: RGB numpy array
- Locking: asyncio.Lock (thread-safe)
- Capture: On-demand at "watch ruby"
- No storage: Images not saved

Text-to-Speech:
---------------
- Primary: gTTS (online, high quality)
- Fallback: pyttsx3 (offline, basic)
- Voice: Female (when available)
- Rate: Slightly slower for seniors

================================================================================
SAFETY & LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

Medical Disclaimer:
-------------------
❌ Ruby CANNOT accurately identify medications by appearance
❌ Ruby CANNOT replace healthcare professionals
❌ ALWAYS verify pill identification with pharmacist/doctor
❌ NEVER take medication based solely on Ruby's identification

What Ruby CAN Do:
-----------------
✅ Read labels on bottles
✅ Provide general health information
✅ Help see small text better
✅ Describe what she sees
✅ Answer questions with available knowledge

Vision Limitations:
-------------------
• Image quality depends on lighting, focus, camera resolution
• Small text might be hard to read
• AI can make mistakes - always double-check important info
• Cannot access real-time data or personal medical records


================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

"Hey Ruby" doesn't respond:
---------------------------
→ Check microphone connected
→ Speak clearly and at moderate volume
→ Check STT initialized in console

"Watch Ruby" says camera not ready:
------------------------------------
→ Check face detection is running
→ Make sure camera is connected
→ Restart server: python main.py

Responses are slow:
-------------------
→ Vision (GPT-4o) takes 2-5 seconds (normal)
→ Text (GPT-3.5) takes 1-2 seconds (normal)
→ Check internet connection speed

Wrong transcription:
--------------------
→ Speak more clearly
→ Reduce background noise
→ Move closer to microphone
→ Check microphone quality
