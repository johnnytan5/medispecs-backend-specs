Problem: "Camera not available"
Fix: Set FACE_DETECTION_ENABLED=True in config.py

Problem: "No frames"
Fix: Wait 2-3 seconds for camera initialization

Problem: Can't connect
Fix: 1. Check server is running
     2. Check URL is correct
     3. Check firewall settings

Problem: Stream is laggy
Fix: 1. Lower quality in streaming.py (80→60)
     2. Reduce FPS (1/15→1/10)
